# Launch checklist

- [ ] Cloudflare account, Worker project, domain, DNS, and canonical `www` redirect confirmed
- [ ] Production Turnstile site/secret keys configured and verified server-side
- [ ] Lead email/webhook/CRM destination and failure alerting connected
- [ ] Rate-limit binding configured and tested
- [ ] Authentic project photos and attributable reviews supplied and approved
- [ ] Certifications, licensing, insurance relationships, and any response-time claims independently verified
- [ ] Legal review of privacy policy, terms, consent copy, and future SMS workflow
- [ ] Decide when `emergencySupport24x7` may be enabled; do not enable before call coverage is operational
- [ ] Analytics provider/ID and consent requirements approved
- [ ] Old URL redirects, 404, canonical host, and no broken links tested in production
- [ ] Search Console verified; sitemap submitted; post-launch crawl and conversion monitoring scheduled
- [ ] Approve or replace the hand-masked extraction-tool photograph with a clean isolated `EX-01` asset
- [ ] Visually approve all eleven hero stages, reverse scroll, mobile composition, and reduced-motion mode in production browsers
- [ ] Inspect the masked van edge at production size; replace its sprite if the source-frame crop is visible
