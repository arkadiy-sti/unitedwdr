# Product strategy

Primary user: a stressed Bay Area property owner who needs clear next steps on a phone. Primary outcome: a qualified call to (408) 385-4892; secondary outcome: a short service request.

Experience principle: **Wow → understand → trust → call.** The cinematic house earns attention, while plain-language service, process, geography, and contact content remain fully indexable without JavaScript.

Verified facts only. No public 24/7, certification, insurance, award, response-time, review, or project claim ships until the business confirms it. Real project photography and reviews replace clearly non-project conceptual imagery later.

The quick form asks name, phone, ZIP/city, incident type, timing, and a short description. “Project start date” becomes “When did the damage happen?” because elapsed time is operationally clearer during a water loss.
