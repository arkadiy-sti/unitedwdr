# SEO strategy

The former Squarespace sitemap exposed `/home`, `/services`, `/about`, `/contact`, `/contact-1`, `/blogs`, and three blog posts. The homepage had the placeholder title “Your Site Title,” no meta description, and headings focused on water mitigation and structural drying in Santa Clara/Bay Area. Those useful topics are retained and improved; obsolete URLs receive permanent redirects.

Every indexable page gets a unique title, description, canonical, logical H1, Open Graph/Twitter text, breadcrumb markup where useful, and centralized Organization/HomeAndConstructionBusiness or Service schema. No rating, opening-hours, 24/7, licensing, certification, or insurance schema is emitted without verification.

San Jose and Santa Clara are the primary geo targets. Four location pages ship with genuinely distinct homeowner guidance; the remaining service-area cities are crawlable through the hub, not doorway pages. Sitemap and robots files use the canonical `https://www.unitedwdr.com` origin.
