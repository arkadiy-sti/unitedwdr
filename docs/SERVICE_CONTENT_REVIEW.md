# Service content review

The eight service pages distinguish cleanup, extraction, drying, and broader restoration rather than using these terms interchangeably. Copy describes conditional field decisions, not a fixed scope or a guarantee that every service is performed on every project.

## Editorial basis

- [ANSI/IICRC S500 overview](https://iicrc.org/s500/) — water-damage assessment, materials, drying, documentation, and safety. The published overview describes the 2021 fifth edition; the standard itself is copyrighted and was not reproduced here.
- [ANSI/IICRC S520 overview](https://iicrc.org/s520/) — mold-remediation scope, containment, material decisions, and verification. The current overview identifies the 2024 fourth edition.
- [EPA flood-cleanup guidance](https://www.epa.gov/emergencies-iaq/flood-cleanup-protect-indoor-air-and-your-health) — floodwater contamination, sorting porous materials, cleaning, and complete drying.
- [California Department of Public Health mold guidance](https://www.cdph.ca.gov/Programs/CCDPHP/DEODC/OHB/HESIS/Pages/mold.aspx) — moisture source control, physical cleaning/removal, worker protection, and avoiding chemical-only remediation.
- [California lead-professional guidance](https://www.cdph.ca.gov/Programs/CCDPHP/DEODC/CLPPB/pages/lrchire_lead_prof.aspx), [Cal/OSHA asbestos construction requirements](https://www.dir.ca.gov/title8/1529.html), and [Cal/OSHA respiratory protection](https://www.dir.ca.gov/title8/5144.html) — relevant when older finishes or respiratory hazards are encountered.

## Claims deliberately avoided

No claim that UNITED is IICRC-certified, fully compliant with every California regulation, licensed for plumbing/roofing/reconstruction, qualified for asbestos or lead abatement, or able to guarantee a mold-free or sterile property. These require business verification and project-specific review.

## Before launch

Have a California restoration operations lead review the described equipment, sewage handling, waste-disposal workflow, and mold-remediation capabilities against UNITED's actual procedures. Have legal/licensing counsel review any public compliance claim before one is added. Confirm service-specific scope, subcontractor relationships, and permit pathways. Recheck referenced standards for revisions during periodic content maintenance.
